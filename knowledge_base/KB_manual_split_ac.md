## Error codes
| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| E1 | Indoor unit temperature sensor fault |
| E2 | Outdoor unit communication failure |
| E3 | Refrigerant leak detected (low-pressure cut-off) |
| E4 | Compressor overload / high current |
| E5 | Indoor fan motor fault |
| E6 | Outdoor unit high-pressure protection tripped |

## Troubleshooting
- **Not cooling:** clean the indoor filters if dirty, confirm the set temperature, and make sure the outdoor unit is not blocked. If **E3** is shown, this is a refrigerant leak — **do not attempt to recharge the unit yourself.**
- **Water leaking from the indoor unit:** the condensate drain pipe is clogged; clear the drain line.
- **Unit trips the breaker / E4:** compressor overload. Power the unit off and contact a technician; do not keep switching it back on.
- **E6 (high-pressure protection):** the outdoor unit pressure is too high. Ensure the outdoor unit has clear airflow and the condenser coil is clean. If it keeps recurring, contact a technician.

## Safety
- R-32 is mildly flammable. Any work on the sealed refrigerant circuit must be done by a **certified refrigerant technician** — never by the customer or an L1 agent.
- If a **gas smell or hissing** is noticed near the unit: switch off at the breaker, ventilate the area, keep away from flames or sparks, and call a certified technician immediately. Do not operate the unit.

## Maintenance
- Clean the indoor filters every 2 weeks.
- Have the unit professionally serviced once a year.
