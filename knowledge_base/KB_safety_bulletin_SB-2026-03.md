# Safety Bulletin SB-2026-03  (Authoritative — supersedes manual)

**Issued:** 2026-03-15
**Product:** Zenith AquaWash ZWM-70FL
**Applies to:** units manufactured before January 2025 (serial prefixes ZWM70-23xx and ZWM70-24xx)
**Subject:** Handling of error F4 (water-heater overheating)

**Instruction:** For affected units, do **NOT** reset and restart the cycle after an F4 fault. A defect in the early heater batch can cause the heater to overheat, and re-running the cycle poses a fire risk.

Correct action: power off, unplug the machine, and arrange replacement of the heater assembly by an authorised technician.

**This bulletin supersedes the F4 instruction ("allow to cool and restart") in the ZWM-70FL user manual for all affected units.**
