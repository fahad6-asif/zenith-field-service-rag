# Zenith FrostGuard Double-Door Refrigerator — Service Manual (extract)

**Model:** ZRF-350DD (350 L)
**Refrigerant:** R-600a (isobutane — flammable)
**Document:** User & Service Manual, Rev. 2024-01

This model has no error-code display; diagnose by symptom.

## Troubleshooting
- **Not cooling (interior light works):** check the thermostat setting, check the condenser coils at the back for dust, confirm the door seals close fully, and confirm the unit is not in a defrost cycle. If it still does not cool, contact a technician.
- **Excessive frost in the freezer:** usually a door left ajar, a worn door gasket, or a defrost fault.
- **Water pooling inside:** the defrost drain is blocked; clear the drain channel.
- **Noise / vibration:** confirm the fridge is level; a low compressor hum is normal.

## Safety
- R-600a is flammable. No open flames near the unit. Any refrigerant or sealed-system service must be done by a qualified technician only.
